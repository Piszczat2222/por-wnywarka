# AltPik Pinterest — Promoted Pins checklist

Do NOT run ads in week 1. Use this after 10–14 days of organic posting.

## When to start

- [ ] At least 10–14 days of organic pins in one cluster
- [ ] Analytics reviewed (saves + outbound clicks)
- [ ] 1–2 pins clearly outperform the rest (relative, even if numbers are small)
- [ ] Landing article loads fast; Amazon CTAs work
- [ ] Profile avatar + bio + website set

## First test setup

- [ ] Campaign objective: Traffic (outbound to AltPik article — NOT direct Amazon)
- [ ] Creative: only the top 1–2 winning pins (same or near-identical graphic)
- [ ] Budget: $5–10/day for 7 days on ONE pin first
- [ ] Audience: Beauty / Skincare / Self-care interests (or Home if home cluster won)
- [ ] Destination: https://altpik.com/articles/<winning-slug> (no trailing slash)

## After 7 days

- [ ] Kill losers (high spend, weak CTR / outbound)
- [ ] Keep or raise budget on the winner only
- [ ] Do not launch a second cluster’s ads until the first test finishes

## What not to promote

- Fresh pins with no organic signal
- Tech comparison articles with no Pinterest saves
- 5+ creatives at once “to see what sticks”

## Weekly review (every Sunday)

1. Open Pinterest Analytics
2. Note top 3 pins by saves and outbound clicks
3. Add winners to “ads candidates” below
