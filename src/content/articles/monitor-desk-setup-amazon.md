### Tips that actually matter

1. Put the top of the screen at or just below eye level.  
2. Clamp the monitor arm to a solid desk edge — hollow tops need a reinforcement plate.  
3. Route cables through the arm and sleeve the same day you mount.  
4. Use the light bar aimed at the desk, not into your eyes.

### What to skip

Don't stack books under a heavy monitor forever. Don't buy a hub that does not match your laptop's USB-C power needs. Don't ignore glare and keep cranking screen brightness.
