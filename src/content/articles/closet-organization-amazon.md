### Tips that actually matter

1. Swap to slim velvet hangers first — instant rod capacity.  
2. Vacuum bags for off-season only; label before storing.  
3. Over-door hooks work for robes and bags when rods are full.  
4. Shelf dividers beat one tall sweater avalanche.

### What to skip

Don't buy more hangers before clearing what you never wear. Don't vacuum-seal daily clothes you need this week. Don't ignore drawer dividers if the dresser is the real mess.
