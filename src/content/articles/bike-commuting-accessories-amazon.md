### Tips that actually matter

1. Charge lights Sunday night with your phone.  
2. Lock frame + wheel through a solid rack point.  
3. Check tire PSI weekly — soft tires feel like weak legs.  
4. Angle the mirror before the first busy intersection.

### What to skip

Don't ride without a rear light after sunset. Don't use a cable-only lock in a theft-heavy city. Don't mount the phone where a face-plant eats it.
