### Tips that actually matter

1. Do kitchen cabinets and unused outlets the same afternoon — don't wait for the first cabinet raid.  
2. Anchor dressers before drawers become climbing ladders.  
3. Test stove knob covers on your specific range — fit varies by brand.  
4. Use hardware-mount guidance for top-of-stair gates; pressure mounts are better for doorways.

### What to skip

Don't buy decorative outlet plates instead of plug covers. Don't skip anti-tip straps because the dresser "feels heavy." Don't rely on a single cabinet lock for a whole kitchen — the 8-pack exists for a reason.
