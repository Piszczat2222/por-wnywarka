### Tips that actually matter

1. Grind right before brew — the Cuisinart only helps if beans aren't pre-ground days ago.  
2. Weigh 1:16 coffee:water as a starting ratio on the Maestri scale.  
3. Cosori: use ~195–205°F for light/medium pour-over; don't always default to boil.  
4. Store beans in the Veken canister, not the open bag with a clip.

### What to skip

Don't buy the Normcore tamper without an espresso machine. Don't stock three filter sizes on day one — match your V60 (02 here). Don't chase a $300 grinder before you own a scale.

### The bottom line

The best **Amazon coffee gadgets** fix grind, weight, and temperature — not counter clutter. Start with the **Cuisinart grinder** or **Zulay frother**, confirm today's prices, then add kettle and V60 when you're ready for pour-over.
