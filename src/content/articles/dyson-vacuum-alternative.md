## Is There a Real Dyson V15 Knockoff on Amazon?

Searching for a **Dyson V15 knockoff** on Amazon? There is no exact clone — Dyson's optic dust detection and Hyperdymium motor engineering are proprietary. What you will find are **budget cordless sticks** that solve the same apartment job for a fraction of the price.

We pin **Dyson V15 Detect Origin** ([Amazon listing](https://amzn.to/3TDWm3n), ASIN `B0GTC14BFW`) at **$839.99**, and **JELLYPIG X100** ([listing](https://amzn.to/4fU8FAc), ASIN `B0F1N21NKM`) at **$139.99**.

## Why Dyson V15 Detect Origin Still Commands the Price

The **V15 Detect Origin** is the aspirational cordless vacuum: Fluffy Optic revealing hidden dust, a piezo sensor that adapts power, an LCD that counts particles, and a **Hyperdymium** motor rated at **240 Air Watts** (~125k rpm). If you have thick carpets, multiple pets, and zero patience for weak suction, Dyson earned its reputation — at **$839.99**.

## How We Compared These Two

We compared both on **suction metrics**, **pet hair tools**, **runtime claims**, **bin/filtration**, **weight**, and **price**. We also cross-check related cordless picks in our [Shark vacuum alternative](/articles/shark-vacuum-alternative) guide.

**Note:** Air Watts (AW), motor watts (W), and kilopascals (kPa) are **not interchangeable** — treat seller claims and Dyson AW ratings as different yardsticks.

Comparison criteria:

- **Suction**: Dyson **240 AW** Hyperdymium vs X100 **55KPA / 650W** brushless claim
- **UI / dust sense**: Fluffy Optic + piezo + LCD vs LED touch screen
- **Pet hair**: Digital Motorbar de-tangle vs anti-tangle brush
- **Runtime**: up to **60 min** (Dyson, mode-dependent) vs up to **70 min** low / no-load claim (X100)
- **Bin / filter**: ~0.2 gal + sealed cartridge filtration vs **1.8 L** + 8-layer HEPA
- **Weight**: **6.8 lb** vs **6.3 lb** (X100 self-stands)
- **Price**: pinned **$839.99** vs **$139.99** — confirm live before checkout

## Why JELLYPIG X100 Is Our Budget Pick

**JELLYPIG X100** delivers what most apartments actually need: cordless freedom, a large **1.8 L** cup, anti-tangle pet-hair cleaning, an LED touch screen, and a self-standing body — at **$139.99**, with seller claims of **55KPA / 650W** and up to **70 minutes** on low.

You won't get Fluffy Optic dust reveal, piezo auto-ramp, or Dyson's 240 AW rating. You will get a home that looks cleaned without draining the renovation budget.

## What You Give Up (and What You Keep)

You give up optic dust reveal, LCD particle proof, sealed Dyson filtration, and the refined Hyperdymium motor. Max-mode runtime still shortens on both machines, and thick wall-to-wall carpet may need slower passes on the budget stick.

What you keep is **cordless convenience**, a **bigger bin**, **stair-friendly weight** (6.3 lb), and roughly **$700** for furniture, plants, or literally anything besides a vacuum. For studios and one-bedrooms, that's the right trade.

## The Bottom Line

Thick carpets, severe allergies, or optic dust obsession? Buy **Dyson V15 Detect Origin** at **$839.99** (**240 AW**). Apartments, hard floors, and budget pet hair? Grab **JELLYPIG X100** at **$139.99** (**55KPA / 650W** claim).
