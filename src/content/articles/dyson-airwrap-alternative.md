Our pin is **Complete Long for Straight to Wavy Hair — Ceramic Pink**. Kit weight on Amazon: about **3.1 kg**.

## Why Shark FlexStyle HD431BR Is Our Pick

**Shark FlexStyle HD431BR** (Mocha Silver) starts as a compact dryer (~**1.5 lb**, **8 ft** cord) and twists into a multi-styler: **2× 1.25″ Auto-Wrap** Coanda curlers, oval brush, styling concentrator, **4 heat / 3 airflow** plus Cool Shot on the Amazon title, and temperature regulation marketed at **1,000× per second**. **1,600W**, **120V**, **2-year** limited warranty — at **$229.99**.
