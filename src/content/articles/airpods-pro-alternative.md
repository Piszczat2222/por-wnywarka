## Why AirPods Pro 3 Still Dominate

Apple's **AirPods Pro 3** ([Amazon listing](https://amzn.to/3TCOkYI), ASIN `B0FQFB8FMG`) reset the in-ear bar again: up to **2× more active noise cancellation** than AirPods Pro 2, upgraded acoustics, spatial audio, Live Translation, heart-rate sensing, and hearing-aid features where supported. We pin them at the **list price of $249.00** (no discount) so the comparison stays honest when Amazon runs sales.

If you live inside Apple's walled garden, use Find My constantly, and want the newest Pro-only tricks, they're still the default recommendation. They're not just earbuds — they're an extension of the iPhone experience.

## How We Compared These Two

We compared both earbuds on **ANC**, **sound**, **battery**, **comfort**, and **ecosystem value**. The budget pick is the same EarFun we use in our [Beats Fit Pro alternative](/articles/beats-fit-pro-alternative) — pinned here from [this Amazon listing](https://amzn.to/4hOqd30).

Key comparison points:

- **Noise cancellation**: AirPods Pro 3 lead on cabin rumble and "world's best in-ear ANC" claims; EarFun covers offices, gyms, and daily transit
- **Audio path**: EarFun offers Snapdragon Sound, aptX Lossless, and LDAC; Apple focuses on spatial audio and H2-class tuning inside its ecosystem
- **Battery**: EarFun claims up to **52 hours** total playtime
- **Extras**: Pro 3 Live Translation / health features vs EarFun multipoint-friendly daily drivers
- **Price**: pinned **$249.00** (AirPods Pro 3 list) vs **$79.99** (EarFun Air Pro 4) — confirm live before checkout

## Why EarFun Air Pro 4 Is Our Budget Pick

The **EarFun Air Pro 4** (ASIN `B0D5M9SH1X`, pinned at **$79.99**) packs adaptive hybrid ANC, Qualcomm Snapdragon Sound, aptX Lossless, LDAC hi-res, Bluetooth 5.4, AI call mics, and in-ear detection — a rare combo under AirPods money.

They won't beat Pro 3 on airplane-mode silence or Apple's spatial / translation stack. For commuting, gym sessions, and daily calls, the gap is much smaller than a ~$169 difference suggests.

## What You Give Up (and What You Keep)

You give up Apple's strongest in-ear ANC, spatial audio polish, Live Translation, heart-rate / hearing-aid features, Find My, and one-tap Apple handoff. The case feels lighter and less refined, and you'll want the EarFun app for full EQ.

What you keep is **real hybrid ANC**, hi-res codecs, long total playtime, and a price that doesn't panic you when a bud disappears in the locker room. Android users especially benefit — you're not subsidizing Apple-only features you can't fully use.

## The Bottom Line

iPhone power users who want Pro 3's ANC and Apple Intelligence-adjacent features should buy **AirPods Pro 3** at **$249.00**. Everyone else — especially Android owners and budget commuters — should grab **EarFun Air Pro 4** at **$79.99**.
