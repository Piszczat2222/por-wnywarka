### Tips that actually matter

1. Label bins and headphones before the first week — lost-and-found starts day two.  
2. Buy EXPO and laminator pouches before August price bumps.  
3. Put the visual timer where every student can see the red disk.  
4. Charge the voice amp Sunday night if you have gym or outdoor duty Monday.

### What to skip

Don't buy a single 4-pack of markers and expect it to last a month. Don't wait on a district document camera if an overhead phone mount unblocks tomorrow's lesson. Don't skip magnetic hooks for tape that peels paint.
