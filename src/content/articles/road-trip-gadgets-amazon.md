### Tips that actually matter

1. Check tire pressure cold, before you roll out of the driveway.  
2. Keep the NOCO charged — a dead jump pack is just a brick.  
3. Pack the cooler with ice packs against one wall so drinks stay accessible.  
4. Put first aid where a passenger can reach it without unloading the trunk.

### What to skip

Don't bring a full mini-fridge for a day trip — the soft cooler wins on space. Don't buy a 2000W inverter for phone/laptop charging. Don't leave the jump starter buried under luggage.
