### What to skip

Don't buy ten props on day one — start with **mat + blocks + strap**. Skip a second stretch strap if the CTRL Sports loops already cover hamstrings and shoulders. Hold Florensi wheels until the beginner trio feels natural; a $65 3-pack is overkill before you've finished a week of flows. Skip barefoot reformer classes if floors are polished — get Ozaiic grip socks first. A zip mat bag is optional when the Tumaz sling ($6.99) already carries the Amazon Basics mat. Wipe and hang the mat after sweaty sessions so it doesn't smell like a locker room.
