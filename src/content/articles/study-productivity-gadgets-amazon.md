### What to skip

Don't buy Loop earplugs if you study with music — get noise-canceling headphones instead. Skip the Rocketbook if you like keeping physical notes; the PAPERAGE journal is $20 cheaper and doesn't require a special pen. Hold the BenQ ScreenBar if your desk has a regular lamp with adjustable brightness. Skip the Anker hub if your laptop has enough ports. Don't buy the dry erase surface if you rent and your landlord is strict — test a small piece first.
