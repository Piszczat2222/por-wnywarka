### Tips that actually matter

1. Zip mesh bags closed — half-open bags defeat the purpose.  
2. Three dryer balls beat a mountain of disposable sheets.  
3. Treat stains the same day; pens work before heat sets them.  
4. Fold on the board once, then muscle memory takes over.

### What to skip

Don't overfill mesh bags until zippers burst. Don't iron synthetics on linen heat. Don't buy a drying rack you cannot store in a closet.
