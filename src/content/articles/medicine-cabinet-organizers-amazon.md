### Tips that actually matter

1. Label bottles when you refill — not when you are sick.  
2. Lazy Susan for deep cabinets; tier risers for shallow ones.  
3. Travel pill case lives in the go-bag, not the cabinet.  
4. First-aid kit gets its own bin so bandages are not buried.

### What to skip

Don't keep expired meds because the cabinet is full anyway. Don't mix daily pills without AM/PM slots. Don't skip labels and guess which bottle is which.
