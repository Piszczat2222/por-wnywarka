### Care tips that actually matter

1. Rinse after ocean or pool, then pat dry.  
2. Sleep in flat-backs only — take heavy stacks off at night.  
3. Keep chains in a soft pouch so they don’t knot in the beach tote.  
4. If plating turns green or flaky, retire it; replacement is cheaper than repair at this price.

### What to skip

Don’t buy both LOLIAS and Jstyle on day one — pick one flat-back set. Don’t swim all afternoon in the thinnest gold plating if you want it to last the week. Don’t force Eiito sizers onto rings wider than ~7 mm.
