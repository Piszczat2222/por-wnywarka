### What to avoid

Skip loud art and bold rugs unless you have seen the place. Do not buy a full kitchen appliance set they did not ask for. Wine charms and novelty shot glasses look like filler next to a real board or opener. If they rent, skip permanent fixtures — stick to throws, plugs, towels, and mats they can take to the next place.
