### What to avoid

Skip fragile glass ornaments for mail-order gifts — they arrive shattered. Avoid strong perfume if you do not know sensitivities. Do not buy adult clothing as a "stuffer" — it never fits the stocking and rarely fits the person. Skip proprietary refill systems unless you know they already own the device.
