### Tips that actually matter

1. Brush before bathing — wet mats tighten and hurt more.  
2. Dematting: short sessions, not one marathon; stop if the skin reddens.  
3. Epiotic: fill the canal, massage the base, let them shake — don't shove Q-tips deep.  
4. Aquapaw: start on low flow; many dogs panic at full shower pressure.

### What to skip

Don't buy the grooming table for a 10 lb short-coat dog you bathe twice a year. Don't use human shampoo. Don't dematt aggressively near ears and armpits without patience (or a pro).
