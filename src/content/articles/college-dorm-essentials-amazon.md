### Tips that actually matter

1. Ship the surge strip and shower caddy to campus before you drive — week-one chaos hits hard.  
2. Raise the bed before unpacking bins so you're not rebuilding the room twice.  
3. Check housing rules on surge protectors vs extension cords — strip with surge protection is usually the safe bet.  
4. Empty and dry the shower caddy after each trip or it becomes a science experiment.

### What to skip

Don't bring a full-size fridge without asking housing (and your roommate). Don't buy decorative storage before the over-door organizer. Don't skip the surge strip for a cheap ungrounded splitter.
