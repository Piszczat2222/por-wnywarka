### Tips that actually matter

1. Don't run the Cosori without a light wipe of the basket — leftover oil smokes next batch.  
2. Instant Pot: sauté first, then pressure — better flavor than dump-and-start.  
3. Lodge: dry thoroughly after wash; a thin oil coat keeps seasoning alive.  
4. Weigh coffee and flour on the Etekcity — cups lie, grams don't.

### What to skip

Don't buy both Cosori and Instant Pot on day one unless the budget is there — pick your dinner style. Don't stack five silicone spatula sets. Don't use glass meal-prep containers on a bare broiler rack.
