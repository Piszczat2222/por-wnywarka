When we say best sellers, we mean categories people reorder or replace after moving — not the orange badge that rotates hourly.

| Morning routine | Electric kettle or 12-cup coffee maker |

- **Review volume** — categories with tens of thousands of ratings
- **Weeknight use** — tools that show up in real dinner routines
- **Gift-ability** — appliances and pans people ask for, not gizmos
- **Price bands** — mostly under $120, with utensils under $30
