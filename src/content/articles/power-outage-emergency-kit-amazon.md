### Tips that actually matter

1. Charge the power bank weekly so it is not empty when the lights die.  
2. Keep flashlights on each floor — not all in one drawer.  
3. Store batteries with the lantern, not in a random junk drawer.  
4. Fill water jugs before a known storm when possible.

### What to skip

Don't rely on phone flashlight as your only light. Don't burn open candles near curtains. Don't wait until the storm hits to order batteries.
