### Tips that actually matter

1. Pack by category, not by day — cubes make hotel drawers optional.  
2. Hang the toiletry bag on day one so the counter stays clear.  
3. Put the passport wallet in the same pocket every flight.  
4. Compress bulky knits, not jackets you need mid-trip.

### What to skip

Don't free-bag liquids in leaky hotel bottles. Don't check a bag without a luggage tag. Don't bury charging cables under dirty socks.
