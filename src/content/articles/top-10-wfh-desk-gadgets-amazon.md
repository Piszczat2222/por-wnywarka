### Tips that actually matter

1. Raise the laptop to eye level before buying more monitors — stand first, arm later.  
2. Mount the cable tray before you rearrange the whole desk twice.  
3. Dim the light bar below screen brightness so the monitor stays the brightest thing.  
4. Slide the webcam cover closed between calls — habit beats trust.

### What to skip

Don't buy a $200 docking station if an Anker hub covers your monitors. Don't skip cable management for another decorative plant. Don't expect blue-light glasses to replace sleep and breaks.
