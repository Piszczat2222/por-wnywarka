### Tips that actually matter

1. Clean walls before adhesive mounts — dust kills stick.  
2. Hang the liner inside the curtain so water stays in the tub.  
3. Group vanity items by morning vs night routines.  
4. Measure toilet height before buying over-toilet shelves.

### What to skip

Don't drill into tile for a TP holder when adhesive works. Don't buy a fabric curtain without a liner. Don't stack open bottles on the tub floor "temporarily".
