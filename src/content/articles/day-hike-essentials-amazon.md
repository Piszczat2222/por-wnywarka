### Tips that actually matter

1. Start hydrated; electrolytes mid-hike beat bonking at the trailhead parking lot.  
2. Tape hot spots at the first rub — don't wait for a full blister.  
3. Pack the rain shell even if the forecast looks “fine.”  
4. Tell someone your route and ETA; the headlamp is plan B, not plan A.

### What to skip

Don't bring overnight camping bulk for a 6-mile day. Don't hike in cotton gym socks. Don't rely on phone flashlight instead of a headlamp when both hands are on poles.
