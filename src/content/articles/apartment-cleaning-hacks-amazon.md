### What to skip

Don't buy the Bissell Little Green if you have all hard floors — a mop and Bar Keepers Friend handle that. Skip the FURemover broom if you don't have pets; a regular broom works fine for dust. Hold Angry Mama if your microwave has a steam-clean button. Don't use Magic Eraser on glossy paint — it's a fine abrasive that can dull shine. Don't use pumice stone on colored porcelain without testing a hidden spot first.
