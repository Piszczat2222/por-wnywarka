### Tips that actually matter

1. Mount the mic on a boom arm away from the keyboard.  
2. Ring light behind the camera, not shining into your eyes.  
3. Green screen needs even lighting to key out cleanly.  
4. Capture card users: check USB bandwidth on your hub.

### What to skip

Don't buy a macro pad before fixing audio. Don't stream with overhead room light as your only source. Don't run HDMI capture through a random unpowered hub.

The best **Amazon streaming setup essentials** fix audio and lighting first. Start with a **USB mic**, **ring light**, and **pop filter**, confirm today's prices, then add a capture card or green screen for production value.
