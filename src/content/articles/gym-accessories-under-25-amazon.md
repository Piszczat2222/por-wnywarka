### Tips that actually matter

1. Use the lightest Fit Simplify bands for activation — save the heaviest for lateral walks, not as a deadlift substitute.  
2. Straps help overload pulls; still train raw grip some sets.  
3. Rinse the BlenderBottle the same day or it smells forever.  
4. Wipe equipment with the microfiber towel — gyms notice, partners notice.

### What to skip

Don't buy both gloves and silicone pads on day one. Don't start with ankle weights heavier than you need — this BAGAIL pair is a light 1 lb each. Don't treat the ab wheel as a day-one max-out; short controlled rolls beat face-plants.
