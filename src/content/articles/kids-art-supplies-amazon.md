### Tips that actually matter

1. Smock first on paint day — saves one shirt meltdown.  
2. Store everything in one caddy so setup takes two minutes.  
3. Blunt scissors only until kids are steady with cuts.  
4. Play-Doh in a bin, not loose in the carpet.

### What to skip

Don't buy permanent markers for kids craft time. Don't skip the smock because paint is washable — tables exist. Don't mix school scissors with kitchen shears.
