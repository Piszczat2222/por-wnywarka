### Tips that actually matter

1. Foam first, then contact wash — less grit dragged across clear coat.  
2. Dedicate light-color towels to paint and dark ones to wheels/tires.  
3. Clay only on clean, lubricated paint — never dry.  
4. Empty the car trash can weekly or the “detail” never starts clean.

### What to skip

Don't buy a foam cannon without a compatible pressure washer. Don't clay a dirty car. Don't expect one microfiber towel to dry a whole SUV.
