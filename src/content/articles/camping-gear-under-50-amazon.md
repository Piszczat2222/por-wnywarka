### What to skip

Skip a second lantern once you have the 1800LM 2-pack. Don’t buy both LifeStraw and Sawyer unless you want straw **and** bottle screw-on — pick one filter style first ($13.49–$16.99). Check campfire rules before relying on the ferro rod; many parks ban open flame. Confirm EN417 canister fit before packing the stove. Skip the $78 down blanket if you’re strictly under $50 — nine other picks already cover a full weekend.
