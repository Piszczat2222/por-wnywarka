### Tips that actually matter

1. Don't cover the entire basket floor with parchment — leave edge gaps for airflow.  
2. Mist oil, don't soak — TrendPlain beats dumping from the bottle.  
3. Pull the basket out to flip with tongs; reaching in hot is how knuckles burn.  
4. Degrease with Weiman when fries stop crisping — it's usually gunk, not a broken fryer.

### What to skip

Don't buy three liner styles on day one — silicone + one parchment pack is enough. Don't force an 8″ rack into a small 3-qt basket. Don't run the fryer empty to “test” parchment; follow the pack's heat guidance.
