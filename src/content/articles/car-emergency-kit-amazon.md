### Tips that actually matter

1. Put cables and the inflator in the organizer now — not after the first dead battery.  
2. Mount the escape tool where a belted driver can reach it.  
3. Charge the flashlight quarterly; lithium packs die quietly in trunks.  
4. Check first-aid dates at every oil change.

### What to skip

Don't buy a giant novelty “150-piece emergency kit” of plastic tools you'll never use. Don't bury triangles under grocery bags. Don't rely on a phone alone without a car charger.
