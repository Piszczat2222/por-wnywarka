## Build a Trunk Kit Before You Need It

Roadside emergencies are boring until they are not. A dead battery at night or a soft tire on a highway shoulder turns into an expensive tow if the trunk is empty. This Amazon list covers the **core car emergency kit** most drivers actually use — power, air, light, first aid, and visibility.

Skip the 80-piece novelty kits full of useless plastic. Buy a few proven tools and keep them in a **collapsible organizer** so they stay findable.

### Pack in this order

| Priority | Why |
|---|---|
| Jumper cables | Most common failure mode |
| Tire inflator | Soft tires before full flats |
| Flashlight + triangles | Night visibility |
| First aid + phone power | Injuries and calling for help |
| Weather / rescue tools | Rain, cold, rare escape needs |

### Seasonal add-ons

Winter: ice scraper, extra blanket, small bag of cat litter for traction. Summer road trips: extra water and a sunshade. Check the kit every oil change — batteries in flashlights die quietly.
