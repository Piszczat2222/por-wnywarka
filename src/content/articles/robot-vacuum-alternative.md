## Is There a Cheap Roomba Alternative on Amazon?

Looking for a **Roomba alternative** that still does daily floors? iRobot's **j7+** brings camera smarts and a self-empty dock. The practical Amazon budget pick is **eufy 11S MAX** — thin, quiet, self-charging, and a fraction of the price.

We pin **iRobot Roomba j7+ (7550)** ([Amazon listing](https://amzn.to/4bwavFW), ASIN `B094NYHTMF`) at **$794.95**, and **eufy 11S MAX** ([listing](https://amzn.to/4wJDq1Z), ASIN `B07R295MLS`) at **$169.99** (list **$279.99**).

## Why Roomba j7+ Still Commands the Price

**Roomba j7+** is built for messy real homes: **PrecisionVision** identifies and avoids cords, socks, shoes, and pet waste (backed by iRobot's **P.O.O.P.** replacement promise), **Imprint Smart Mapping** stores multi-level maps and room schedules, and the **Clean Base** auto-empties into enclosed bags for up to **~60 days**. Cleaning stack: **3-Stage** system with **10× Power-Lifting Suction** vs the Roomba 600 series, dual multi-surface rubber brushes, and an edge-sweeping brush. Specs from the listing / iRobot: about **13.3″ × 13.3″ × 3.4″**, ~**7.5 lb**, lithium-ion, listing battery life up to **120 minutes**, Alexa / Google / Siri via the iRobot app. Model **j755020**.

## How We Compared These Two

We compared **navigation**, **suction claims**, **self-empty convenience**, **runtime**, **height / noise**, and **price**.

Comparison criteria:

- **Smarts**: PrecisionVision + Imprint maps + app/voice vs remote-only bump/IR navigation
- **Suction**: 10× vs Roomba 600 (iRobot) vs **2,000 Pa** + BoostIQ (eufy)
- **Emptying**: Clean Base ~60-day bags vs manual washable-filter bin
- **Runtime**: up to **120 min** (j7+ listing) vs up to **100 min** hardwood (11S MAX)
- **Fit**: **3.4″** tall vs **2.85″** slim; eufy claims ~**55 dB**
- **Price**: pinned **$794.95** vs **$169.99** — confirm live before checkout

## Why eufy 11S MAX Is Our Budget Pick

**eufy 11S MAX** (model **T2126114**) is the low-drama daily cleaner: **2.85″** tall to slide under furniture, up to **2,000 Pa** suction, **BoostIQ** that ramps within ~**1.5 seconds**, up to **100 minutes** on hardwood, self-return to HomeBase, infrared obstacle + drop sensing, tempered-glass top, and ~**55 dB** quiet claim — at **$169.99**. No Wi‑Fi, no app: remote and onboard buttons only. Amazon feedback sits around **4.3★** across tens of thousands of ratings.

It won't map rooms or dodge pet waste with a camera. It will run on a schedule, stay quiet, and keep hard floors presentable without an $800 commitment.

## What You Give Up (and What You Keep)

You give up PrecisionVision obstacle AI, smart multi-floor maps, app/voice control, Clean Base auto-empty, and iRobot's pet-waste promise. Cluttered floors with cables and toys still challenge bump-style robots.

What you keep is **daily hair and dust pickup**, **quiet runs**, **self-charging**, and a **thinner body** that reaches more under-sofa dust. For first-time robot buyers, the lower pin removes commitment anxiety.

## The Bottom Line

Pets, clutter, and “empty it for me for two months”? Buy **Roomba j7+** at **$794.95**. Apartments and clear hard floors on a budget? Grab **eufy 11S MAX** at **$169.99**.
