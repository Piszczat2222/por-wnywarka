### Tips that actually matter

1. Label with cook date, not just “chicken.”  
2. Put ice packs against the protein side of the bag.  
3. Weigh once on Sunday — eyeballing portions creeps up by Wednesday.  
4. Keep a utensil set in the bag so you never “forget” again.

### What to skip

Don't buy 40 mismatched plastic deli containers. Don't skip ice packs in summer. Don't expect a kids FunTAINER to hold adult soup portions.
