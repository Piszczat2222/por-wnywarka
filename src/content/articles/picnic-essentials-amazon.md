### Tips that actually matter

1. Put the cooler in the shade under a corner of the blanket.  
2. Spray ankles and cuffs before you sit down.  
3. Pack a trash bag in the same pouch as utensils.  
4. Use lids on tumblers — blankets are not tables.

### What to skip

Don't bring glass wine stems to a windy park. Don't forget bug spray because "it is daytime". Don't leave food scraps for raccoons and ants.
