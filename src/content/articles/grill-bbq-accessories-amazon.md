### Tips that actually matter

1. Temp chicken and pork every time — color lies.  
2. Start charcoal in the chimney before guests arrive.  
3. Oil mats lightly so food releases clean.  
4. Cover the grill once it cools — moisture is the enemy.

### What to skip

Don't use a wire brush that sheds bristles. Don't skip the thermometer "because you know". Don't leave the grill uncovered through a rainy week.
