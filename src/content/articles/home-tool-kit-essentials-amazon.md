### Tips that actually matter

1. Keep the tool box in one known spot — not spread across junk drawers.  
2. Magnetic torpedo level lives on the fridge until shelf day.  
3. Utility knife beats scissors for Amazon boxes.  
4. Flashlight in the box beats phone-in-mouth repairs.

### What to skip

Don't buy a 200-piece kit full of bits you will never use. Don't hang shelves with eyeballing instead of a level. Don't store duct tape somewhere other than the tool box.

The best **Amazon home tool kit essentials** cover hang, measure, cut, and tighten. Start with a **screwdriver set**, **hammer**, and **tape measure**, confirm today's prices, then add a **tool box** so everything stays together.
