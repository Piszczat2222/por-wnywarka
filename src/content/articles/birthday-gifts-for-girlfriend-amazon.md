### Gift presentation tips

1. Print the Spotify frame photo *before* birthday morning — don't make her wait on setup.  
2. Pair flowers with something lasting (tray, tumbler, or sleep set), not roses alone.  
3. For SWOMOG, order her size first; add yours only if you've confirmed.  
4. Stick a short note on *why* this gift — one specific memory beats a long generic card.

### What to skip

Don't buy clothes without her size. Don't force the KODAK printer if she hates desk clutter — go Spotify frame. Don't guess perfume or fine jewelry from a banner ad when this list already covers personal + practical.

### The bottom line

The best **Amazon birthday gifts for your girlfriend** show you pay attention — to her routines, your shared songs, and how she spends evenings. Start with the **Spotify frame** or **satin sleep set**, confirm today's prices, and write one specific note.
