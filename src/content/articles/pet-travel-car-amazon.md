### Tips that actually matter

1. Introduce the harness on short trips before a long haul.  
2. Fill the travel bottle before leaving — rest stops are not always quick.  
3. Calming chews work best given 30–45 minutes before departure.  
4. Barrier + harness beats hoping they stay in the back.

### What to skip

Don't let an unrestrained dog ride in the front seat. Don't use household couch throws that slide every turn. Don't forget bags because the last roll is in the glove box somewhere.
