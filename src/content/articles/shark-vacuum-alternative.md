## Is There a Real Shark Stratos Knockoff on Amazon?

Looking for a **Shark Stratos dupe** on Amazon? There is no exact clone — **Clean Sense IQ**, **DuoClean PowerFins HairPro**, **Odor Neutralizer**, and **HyperVelocity Plus** are Shark proprietary. What you will find are **budget cordless sticks** that cover the same apartment job for less.

We pin **Shark Stratos Cordless IZ862H** ([Amazon listing](https://amzn.to/4weE2vp), ASIN `B0B5JMNGNQ`) at **$349.99**, and **JELLYPIG X100** ([listing](https://amzn.to/4fU8FAc), ASIN `B0F1N21NKM`) at **$139.99** — the same budget pick we use in our [Dyson vacuum alternative](/articles/dyson-vacuum-alternative).

## Why Shark Stratos IZ862H Still Commands the Price

**IZ862H** is Shark's intelligent cordless flagship for mixed floors: infrared **Clean Sense IQ** that auto-boosts for up to **50% better dirt pickup** vs Eco, a **HyperVelocity Plus** motor listed at **309W / 25.2V / 12.26A**, **DuoClean PowerFins HairPro** dual brushrolls, **Odor Neutralizer**, **MultiFLEX** bend-and-store, and **Anti-Allergen Complete Seal** HEPA (**99.9%** of particles **≥0.3 µm**, ASTM F1977). Runtime: up to **60 min** Eco at the hand vac, roughly **40–50 min** in IQ, and **~10–14 min** Boost; charge about **3.5 hours**. Specs from the [Amazon listing](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B5JMNGNQ): **8.9 lb**, **0.72 qt** cup, **10"** path, **~80 dB**, Ash Purple, **5-year** limited warranty.

## How We Compared These Two

We compared **motor metrics**, **dirt sensing / UI**, **brush systems**, **runtime claims**, **bin / filtration**, **weight**, **noise**, and **price**.

**Note:** electrical watts (W), kilopascals (kPa), and ASTM hand-vac suction are **not interchangeable** — treat Shark's HyperVelocity Plus rating and X100's 55KPA claim as different yardsticks.

Comparison criteria:

- **Motor**: Shark **309W · 25.2V · 12.26A** HyperVelocity Plus vs X100 **650W** brushless / **55KPA** claim
- **Sense / UI**: Clean Sense IQ infrared + LED modes vs LED touch screen
- **Pet hair**: DuoClean PowerFins HairPro (self-cleaning) vs anti-tangle brush
- **Runtime**: up to **60 min** Eco (Shark, hand vac) vs up to **70 min** low / no-load (X100)
- **Bin / filter**: **0.72 qt** sealed HEPA vs **1.8 L** 8-layer HEPA
- **Weight**: **8.9 lb** vs **6.3 lb** (X100 self-stands)
- **Noise**: ~**80 dB** vs ~**58–62 dB** claim
- **Price**: pinned **$349.99** vs **$139.99** — confirm live before checkout

## Why JELLYPIG X100 Is Our Budget Pick

**JELLYPIG X100** delivers what most apartments need: cordless freedom, a large **1.8 L** cup, anti-tangle pet-hair cleaning, LED touch screen, self-standing body, **29.6V / 650W** seller specs, up to **70 minutes** on low (no-load, 8×2800 mAh), and ~**4-hour** charge — at **$139.99**. Listing extras include wall mount, 2-in-1 brush + crevice, spare HEPA + sponge, and **24 months** of protection (~**4.6★**, thousands of ratings).

You won't get Clean Sense IQ, PowerFins HairPro, MultiFLEX, or Shark's sealed anti-allergen stack. You will get a home that looks cleaned without the Stratos bill.

## What You Give Up (and What You Keep)

You give up infrared dirt auto-boost, dual PowerFins brushrolls, Odor Neutralizer, MultiFLEX under-furniture reach, and Shark's 5-year warranty. Max-mode runtime still shortens on both machines, and thick wall-to-wall carpet may need slower passes on the budget stick.

What you keep is **cordless convenience**, a **much bigger bin**, **lighter weight** (6.3 vs 8.9 lb), and roughly **$210** for other home upgrades. For studios and one-bedrooms, that's the right trade.

## The Bottom Line

Heavy pets, thick carpets, or Clean Sense IQ + PowerFins obsession? Buy **Shark Stratos IZ862H** at **$349.99**. Apartments, hard floors, and budget pet hair? Grab **JELLYPIG X100** at **$139.99**.
