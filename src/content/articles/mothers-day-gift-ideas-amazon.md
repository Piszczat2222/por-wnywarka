### Gift presentation tips

1. Pair flowers with something lasting (silk pillowcase, kettle, or robot vac) — not roses alone.  
2. Charge the eye mask, Gritin, and Cosori before wrapping so she can use them that day.  
3. Drop a small essential-oil sample next to the InnoGear diffuser.  
4. Write one specific note — her early tea, sore shoulders, or the floors she never stops sweeping.

### Care tips that actually matter

1. Hand-wash or gentle-cycle the ZIMASILK case; air dry — heat wrecks silk.  
2. Empty and wipe the InnoGear tank so mineral film doesn't build up.  
3. Chill the jade roller in the fridge before morning use; clean after oil.  
4. Map no-go zones in the robot app (cords, pet bowls) before the first run.

### What to skip

Don't buy clothing unless you know her size. Don't guess perfume. Don't default to a "World's Best Mom" mug unless that's genuinely her humor. Don't order the robot vac if floors are mostly thick carpet she already hates robots on — check her floor type first.

### The bottom line

The best **Amazon Mother's Day gifts** respect her time — smoother mornings, cozier evenings, or fewer chores. Start with the **ZIMASILK pillowcase** or **heated eye mask**, confirm today's prices, and write one specific note.
