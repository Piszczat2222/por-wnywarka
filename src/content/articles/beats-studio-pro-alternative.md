## Why Beats Studio Pro Still Command the Price

**Beats Studio Pro** is Apple's flagship over-ear: USB-C lossless audio, personalized spatial audio with dynamic head tracking, and adaptive ANC tuned for iPhone users who want the Beats look in the studio and on flights. We pin them at **$349.99** ([Amazon listing](https://amzn.to/4c19EgA), ASIN `B0C8PWSW7T`). You're paying for brand, ecosystem polish, and UltraPlush materials. The bass-forward Beats signature is why a lot of people reach for Studio Pro first.

If you live in Apple Music, plug into USB-C for lossless, and want one pair that handshakes cleanly with iPhone and Mac, that premium can make sense. For Android users — or anyone who will mostly stream Bluetooth on commute — the gap to Space One at **$69.99** is hard to ignore.

## How We Compared These Two

We evaluated **ANC quality**, **sound tuning and codecs**, **battery life with ANC on**, **comfort for long sessions**, and **ecosystem extras**. We also cross-checked the same **Soundcore Space One** pick from our [AirPods Max alternative](/articles/airpods-max-alternative) guide — same over-ear value play, different premium rival (Beats Studio Pro instead of AirPods Max).

Our comparison weighed:

- **ANC**: Beats adaptive ANC for flights and loud transit; Space One hybrid ANC plus voice-reduction modes for offices and cafés
- **Audio path**: Beats USB-C lossless + spatial audio vs Space One LDAC and app EQ (easy to push bass toward Beats territory)
- **Battery**: Beats roughly 24 hours with ANC / up to 40 off; Space One up to **40 hours with ANC on**
- **Build & travel**: Beats premium padding and case vs Space One's lighter foldable plastic build
- **Price**: pinned **$349.99** (Beats) vs **$69.99** (Space One Sky Blue) — confirm live before checkout

## Why Soundcore Space One Is Our Pick

We pinned **Soundcore Space One** (Sky Blue, ASIN `B0C6KKQ7ND`) from [this Amazon listing](https://amzn.to/4fG8KIR) at **$69.99**: hybrid ANC with voice reduction, LDAC hi-res, app control, AI mics, and up to **40 hours with ANC** — travel-ready and about **$280** under Beats Studio Pro.

You won't get Beats branding, USB-C lossless through Apple's preferred path, or spatial audio tricks. You will get comfortable daily headphones, multipoint-friendly Bluetooth, and EQ that lets you chase that Beats bass without the Studio Pro tax.

## What You Give Up (and What You Keep)

You give up Apple's lossless USB-C pipeline, personalized spatial audio, UltraPlush materials, and the social signal of Beats on your head. ANC on the noisiest airplanes isn't class-leading, and the plastic build doesn't feel as refined when you handle both side by side.

What you keep is **real over-ear ANC**, long battery with ANC left on, LDAC hi-res audio, app EQ, and a comfortable foldable fit for multi-hour work sessions. For open offices, commuting, and studying, the daily experience is strong enough that saving ~$280 is hard to ignore.

## The Bottom Line

Apple Music power users who will use USB-C lossless and spatial audio should buy **Beats Studio Pro** at **$349.99**. Everyone else looking for over-ear ANC should grab **Soundcore Space One** at **$69.99** — and if you're weighing Apple's aluminum flagship instead, read our [AirPods Max alternative](/articles/airpods-max-alternative) for the same pick in that context. The Beats tax is still real; Space One is how most people skip most of it.
