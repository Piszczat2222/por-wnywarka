### Tips that actually matter

1. Put the sound machine on before sleep cues break — consistency beats volume.  
2. Stock the portable caddy for living-room changes so you're not hiking to the nursery every hour.  
3. Test the White Hot duck in warm (not scalding) water once so you trust the color change.  
4. Keep NoseFrida filters stocked before cold season — empty boxes appear at the worst time.

### What to skip

Don't buy a WiFi monitor with a paid sleep-analytics plan if HelloBaby covers you. Don't stock five decorative organizers before one portable caddy. Don't skip the $3 bath duck for a fancy digital thermometer you won't use.

### The bottom line

The best **Amazon baby essentials** protect sleep, feeding, and diaper logistics. Start with the **monitor**, **Hatch Go**, and **caddy**, confirm today's prices, then add warmer, Boppy, and NoseFrida as real life starts.
