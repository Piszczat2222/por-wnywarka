### Tips that actually matter

1. Wash the Veken fountain weekly — biofilm is why cats stop using dirty fountains.  
2. Breeze: change pads on schedule; pellets aren't clay scoop litter.  
3. Put the scratch post where the cat already scratches — relocation fails.  
4. Empty Litter Genie before it's packed solid or the seal stops helping.

### What to skip

Don't buy three fish toys on day one — one Potaroma is enough to test interest. Don't skip a tall post for a tiny cardboard scratcher if the couch is already shredded. Don't expect ChomChom to replace vacuuming carpets entirely.

The best **Amazon cat gadgets** protect the furniture, the air, and the cat's water habit. Start with the **Veken fountain** or **Breeze system**, confirm today's prices, then add ChomChom and a scratch post.
