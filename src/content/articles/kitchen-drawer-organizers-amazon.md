### Tips that actually matter

1. Measure drawer width and height before ordering expandable trays.  
2. Spice drawer tier only works if the drawer is deep enough.  
3. Knife inserts beat loose blades rolling on open.  
4. Non-slip liner under trays stops drift.

### What to skip

Don't buy fixed-size organizers without measuring. Don't store knives loose because the block is full. Don't skip liners and wonder why trays slide every week.
