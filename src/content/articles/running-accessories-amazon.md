### Tips that actually matter

1. Size the Fitgriff from the waist chart — too loose = bounce.  
2. Charge the SAWNZC vest before night runs; reflective fabric alone isn't enough in rain.  
3. Roll calves and glutes *after* hard days, not right before a race warm-up.  
4. Apply Body Glide before the first mile of humid long runs — not after you're already raw.

### What to skip

Don't buy both a loaded belt and a full armband on day one — pick one phone carry. Don't start with the Garmin if you're still building the habit; belt + Body Glide fix more quit-points. Don't stretch KT Tape over broken skin.

### The bottom line

The best **Amazon running accessories** remove friction: bounce, chafe, darkness, and next-day stiffness. Start with the **Fitgriff belt** or **Body Glide**, confirm today's prices, then add visibility or recovery as your miles grow.
