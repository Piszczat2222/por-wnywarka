### Tips that actually matter

1. Anchor bands high and low for rows and presses.  
2. Put the mat on a clear wall section so pull-ups have space.  
3. Film one set weekly — form beats ego load.  
4. Roll after sessions, not only when you are already wrecked.

### What to skip

Don't buy five gadgets before you train three times. Don't mount a pull-up bar on a hollow door without checking the manual. Don't skip the mat and destroy your knees on hardwood.
