### Tips that actually matter

1. Label boxes on two sides + the top with the destination room.  
2. Wrap dresser drawers shut before they become projectile clutter.  
3. Slide, don't drag — felt sliders first, ego second.  
4. Keep the utility knife and Sharpies in one pocket all day.

### What to skip

Don't “borrow” kitchen cling wrap instead of stretch film. Don't skip straps and deadlift a fridge. Don't unpack clothes from trash bags for a week — cubes pay for themselves on night one.
