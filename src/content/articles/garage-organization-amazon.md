### Tips that actually matter

1. Hang vertical first — floor space is the scarce resource.  
2. Label bins on day one while motivation is high.  
3. Keep the cord reel mounted near the workbench, not behind the car.  
4. Magnetic lights beat a flashlight between your teeth.

### What to skip

Don't buy more mystery totes without labels. Don't leave bikes leaning where cars park. Don't ignore trip hazards from extension cords.
