### Tips that actually matter

1. Keep jumper cables reachable — not buried under summer gear.  
2. Check tire pressure monthly when temps drop.  
3. Windshield cover saves scrape time on repeat frost mornings.  
4. LED flares beat standing in the lane with a phone flashlight.

### What to skip

Don't rely on one disposable scraper all winter. Don't ignore tire pressure because the TPMS light is annoying. Don't store cables in a wet trunk pocket that corrodes clips.
