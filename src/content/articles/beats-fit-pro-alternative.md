## Why Beats Fit Pro Still Command the Price

The **Beats Fit Pro** nailed the workout earbud formula: wing tips that stay put, punchy bass, and Apple's H1 chip for fast pairing with iPhone. We pin them at **$199.95** ([Sage Gray on Amazon](https://amzn.to/3TUAzo8), ASIN `B09JL65GNM`). You're paying for the Beats logo, ecosystem polish, and a fit system designed for high-movement training. Gym-goers deep in Apple land get instant handoff, Siri access, and ANC that handles treadmill noise well.

Beats also benefits from Apple's R&D in transparency mode and spatial audio — features that matter if you run outdoors and need to hear traffic. For athletes who already own AirPods and want a sport-focused sibling in the ecosystem, that price feels more reasonable.

## Why EarFun Air Pro 4 Is Our Pick

The **EarFun Air Pro 4** (ASIN `B0D5M9SH1X`, pinned at **$53.16**) is our Amazon alternative in the same tier as our [AirPods Pro alternative](/articles/airpods-pro-alternative): adaptive hybrid ANC, Qualcomm Snapdragon Sound, aptX Lossless, LDAC hi-res audio, Bluetooth 5.4, and a secure in-ear fit with multiple tip sizes. Thousands of reviews back it as a daily driver for commuting and workouts.

You won't get Apple's one-tap pairing or Beats branding on the gym floor. You will get long total playtime, customizable sound through the app, and a price that doesn't hurt when you misplace one bud in the locker room. For Android users especially, you're not subsidizing features you can't use.

## What You Give Up (and What You Keep)

You give up Beats' flexible wing-tip design tuned for Apple, H1 chip integration, and the brand signal that comes with Beats on your ears at the gym. ANC on low-frequency rumble isn't quite Beats-level, and fit stability depends more on choosing the right ear tip size.

What you keep is **secure fit for most ear shapes**, real hybrid ANC, hi-res codecs (LDAC / aptX Lossless), sweat resistance, and app EQ. For the average gym session and commute, the listening experience is close enough that saving about **$147** feels obvious.

## The Bottom Line

iPhone users who live in Find My, use Apple Fitness+, and want wing tips engineered for Apple should buy **Beats Fit Pro** at **$199.95**. Everyone else looking for workout ANC on a budget should grab **EarFun Air Pro 4** at **$53.16**. The Beats tax is real; most gym-goers don't need to pay it.
