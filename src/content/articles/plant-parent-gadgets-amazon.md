### What to avoid

Don't buy a full smart planter system before a moisture meter — guessing water kills plants faster than missing an app notification. Skip decorative mid-century stands that tip under a heavy ceramic pot; the adjustable metal stand above is for function. Avoid cheap scissors for propagation — clean Fiskars cuts heal better. Hold off on a humidifier if misters and trays haven't been tried yet for a small collection.
