### Tips that actually matter

1. Hang blackout curtains before baby arrives — testing nap darkness is harder with a newborn.  
2. Put the sound machine on before sleep cues break; consistency beats volume.  
3. Stock the portable caddy for living-room changes so you aren't hiking to the nursery every hour.  
4. Cover outlets early — crawling arrives faster than the registry checklist suggests.

### What to skip

Don't buy a WiFi monitor with a paid sleep-analytics plan if a no-WiFi HelloBaby covers your needs. Don't stock five decorative baskets before vertical door storage. Don't buy specialty diaper-pail bags when Ubbi takes regular trash bags.

The best **Amazon nursery essentials** protect sleep and logistics, not the Instagram feed. Start with **curtains**, **Hatch Go**, and the **HelloBaby monitor**, confirm today's prices, then add caddy, Ubbi, and the glider as the room gets real.
